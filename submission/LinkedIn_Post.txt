Photos keep what a day looked like. I wanted a place for the details I would otherwise forget.

That is what I am building with Schema: a private scrapbook where a photo, a few words and the people around a moment can stay together.

For Kritical Hacks, we have added a TinyFish workflow. You paste a public event page, the agent reads its details, and Schema lets you choose which facts should enter your draft. Your own note stays separate from the public context. Nothing is added automatically.

We ran a live provider test on the PyCon US website and got back its dates, venue and program sections. The code and review flow are implemented, with local save/reopen tests. We are still completing authenticated hosted verification.

The broader idea is to make these pages useful years later: finding the people, places and thoughts that connect different parts of your life.

I would like feedback on this: what is something you wish you had written down alongside an old photo?
