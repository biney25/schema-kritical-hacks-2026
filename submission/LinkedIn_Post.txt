I have photos from days I barely remember now. The photo is still there, but the conversation, the people around it or the reason I took it can disappear.

Schema is a private scrapbook for those details. Add a photo, write a few words and keep the page in your life archive.

For events, we added a TinyFish workflow. Paste the public event website and the agent reads its title, dates, venue and program. You check the returned facts and choose what enters your draft. The source stays attached. Your private notes and photos stay outside the research request.

One of our live TinyFish runs returned PyCon US 2026's dates, venue and program sections in five steps. The repository includes that result and a command to reproduce the research and reviewed-draft workflow.

The next step is a small adult-user pilot to see whether people actually return to the pages they make.
